ULTRON PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ULTRON website may collect, why it is used, and the services that may process it. This policy applies to the current ULTRON authentication website and to any ULTRON features that are enabled in the future, subject to updates to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and a Firebase user ID when you create an ULTRON account.
  • Google sign-in information: when you choose Google Sign-In, Google/Firebase may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier, depending on the permissions and provider response.
  • Authentication information: passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. ULTRON does not intentionally store your plain-text password in its Firestore database.
  • Technical information: Firebase, Google, your browser, hosting provider, or other infrastructure may process technical information such as device, browser, IP address, security events, and diagnostic logs according to their own policies.
  • Future AI information: if ULTRON's AI/voice features are enabled, information you submit to those features, such as prompts or transcribed commands, may be processed by the AI provider used by ULTRON. The exact providers and data flow will be described here before those features are released.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, provide password recovery, maintain sessions, prevent abuse, operate ULTRON features, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

4. Firebase and Google services

ULTRON uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for account authentication and account-related data. Google services may also be used for Google Sign-In and, when enabled, Google AI services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. ULTRON does not control Google's internal processing, security systems, or email-delivery decisions.

5. API keys and security

The Firebase web configuration contains an API key intended to identify the Firebase project; it is not treated as a user password. Any secret Google/Gemini API credential must be kept on a secure server and must not be embedded in browser JavaScript. ULTRON will not intentionally request or display a user's password or private API keys.

6. Data storage

Account records are stored using Firebase services. Data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate an account, provide security, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable obligations. Exact retention periods may depend on the service and feature.

7. Password reset emails

Password-reset emails are delivered through the authentication service. ULTRON cannot guarantee that Gmail or another email provider will place a message in the Inbox; filtering may place it in Spam, Junk, Promotions, or another folder.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to create an ULTRON account or use Google Sign-In. You can stop using the website and may request deletion of account information where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, backups, or legal reasons where permitted or required.

9. Children's and teen users

ULTRON is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for a minor's use of a service, that consent should be obtained. If a parent or guardian believes information was collected improperly, they can contact the site operator to request review and deletion where appropriate.

10. Third-party links and services

ULTRON may link to or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Users should review those policies before providing information directly to a third party.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ULTRON adds features, changes providers, or changes its data practices. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method provided by the ULTRON site operator. Replace this section with your real support email before publishing the site publicly.
